# VIT StudyMate — Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students often keep course PDFs, notes, assignments and practice material in different places. They need a focused tool that can organize their study material and provide module-specific practice and learning support.

## Scope
StudyMate is a terminal-based academic assistant. It stores uploaded course PDFs, retrieves relevant text, provides AI explanations and quizzes from the selected material, records quiz performance, manages assignments and creates study plans.

## Target Users
First-year engineering students who want a simple academic study assistant based on their own course material.

## High-Level Features
- PDF/module management
- Module-grounded AI tutor
- AI quiz generation
- Performance analysis
- Assignment management
- Study planning
- SQLite data storage
